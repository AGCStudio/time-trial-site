Endurance training,
adapted to you.

Time Trial is a mobile app for endurance athletes and their coaches. Plan structured workouts from your phone, follow a plan that adapts when life gets in the way, and understand your training load in every discipline — on any watch.

Build workouts on your phone

Create and edit structured sessions by power, pace, heart rate or effort — or just describe them in plain words — and send them to your watch.

A plan that adapts

Missed a session, slept badly, short on time? Your plan rebalances itself and tells you why. You choose how much to delegate.

Multisport, done right

Swim, bike and run load tracked separately and together, with flexible zones for indoor and outdoor, and every endurance sport covered.

Athletes and coaches

Coaches manage their athletes' calendars, review sessions and approve the plan adjustments. Athletes stay in control of what they share.

Every watch, equally

Designed to work with Apple Watch, Garmin and other devices, with full export of your data whenever you want it.

Your data stays yours

Hosted in the EU, never sold, never used for advertising. Read our privacy policy.

About

Time Trial is an independent project by Amilcare G. Canali, an Italian triathlete and marathon runner, built for athletes who train seriously and want a tool that works for them — not the other way around.

The app is currently in development for iOS and Android, with a closed beta for athletes and coaches planned for 2027.
