Time Trial is a training app for endurance athletes and their coaches. Plan on your phone. Train with the device you already have. Keep what you planned and what you did in one place.

Join the beta See what it does

What it does

Swim, bike, run. And everything around it.

  1. 01

    Any device.

    Activities arrive from your watch, bike computer and phone health app. FIT, TCX and GPX files work too.

  2. 02

    Workouts on the phone.

    Build intervals with targets in pace, power or heart rate. See the expected load before you start. Compare every step with what you actually did.

  3. 03

    Zones with a date.

    Thresholds change during the season. Time Trial keeps them by date, so every session is read with the values that were true that day.

  4. 04

    Recovery counts.

    Sleep, HRV, weight, physiotherapy, massage and mobility sit in the same calendar as your training.

  5. 05

    Your week, at a glance.

    Planned against done, sport by sport. Move a session with one gesture. Repeat a week you liked.

For coaches

All your athletes. One screen.

Plan from the computer, comment from the phone. Apply a week to a whole group. Your athletes see everything in their app, on any watch.

Invite with a code.

The athlete decides what you can see, and either of you can disconnect at any time.

Week templates.

Save a week once. Apply it to one athlete or to a group.

Works offline.

Your athletes' calendars stay readable without a connection.

Your data

Your data stays yours.

Stored in the European Union. Never sold. Never used for advertising. Never used to train AI models. Export it or delete it whenever you want.
