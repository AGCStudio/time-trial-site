Train with any watch.
Plan on your phone.

Time Trial is a training app for endurance athletes and their coaches: swim, bike, run, triathlon and everything in between. One calendar for what you planned and what you did, whichever device recorded it.

In development

Every watch

Garmin, Polar, Suunto, COROS, Wahoo, Hammerhead, Apple Watch and Android devices through Health Connect. Plus FIT, TCX and GPX files.

Structured workouts on the phone

Build intervals with targets in pace, power or heart rate, see the expected load, and compare each step with what you actually did.

Your zones, your history

Thresholds change over the season. Time Trial keeps them by date, so every activity is analysed with the values that were true that day.

Coach mode

Coaches plan and review from the web; athletes see everything on their phone. Data is shared only with the coach you choose.

Recovery counts

Sleep, HRV, weight, physiotherapy, massage and mobility sit in the same calendar as your training.

Your data stays yours

Stored in the European Union. Never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI models.

About

Time Trial is built in Italy by Amilcare G. Canali, an endurance athlete who wanted one place for all his training data and a planning tool that works as well on a phone as on a computer.
