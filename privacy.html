Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Time Trial ("the app", "we") collects, uses and protects your personal data. Time Trial is a mobile and web application for endurance athletes and coaches, currently in development. We wrote it to be readable: if anything is unclear, contact us.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Amilcare G. Canali, sole proprietor, VAT number 09335660966, Via Pietro Bembo 7, 20021 Bollate (MI), Italy.

Contact: privacy@timetrial.io

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, login method (Apple, Google or email), language, country and app preferences (for example the first day of your week).
  • Optional profile details: a profile photo you choose, and your gender (woman, man, non-binary, other with an optional self-description, or prefer not to say). Gender is used only to choose the formulas for physiological estimates; with non-binary, other or no answer we use average values. Both are visible only to you and to the coaches and athletes you are connected with, and you can change or remove them at any time.
  • Athlete profile: sports practised, thresholds and training zones with their dates (e.g. FTP, threshold pace, heart rate zones), goals and races, equipment.
  • Training data: planned and completed workouts, including structure, targets, time, distance, pace, speed, power, cadence, elevation and GPS route.
  • Health and wellness data: heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep, body weight and body composition, perceived effort and how you feel. These are special categories of data under the GDPR and are processed only with your explicit consent.
  • Recovery sessions: physiotherapy, massage or mobility sessions you log, optionally with the name of the professional who treated you. This name is visible only to you and to a coach you connect with.
  • Notes and messages you write in the app or exchange with your coach.
  • Technical data: device type, operating system, app version and crash reports, used only to keep the app working.
  • Web app: in the browser we store only your login session (strictly necessary to keep you signed in; no tracking or advertising cookies).

3. Where the data comes from

You enter some data directly. Other data comes from services you choose to connect, only after you authorise them, and only for the data types you allow:

  • watches and bike computers: Garmin Connect, Polar, Suunto, COROS, Wahoo, Hammerhead;
  • Apple Health and Apple Watch (HealthKit and WorkoutKit) on iPhone; Health Connect on Android;
  • recovery and body data: WHOOP, Oura, Withings;
  • indoor training and rowing: Zwift, Rouvy, MyWhoosh, Concept2 Logbook, FORM swim goggles;
  • activity files you upload (FIT, TCX, GPX) and data archives you export yourself from other services (for example a Strava archive). We do not connect to the Strava API.

Some of these connections are still being enabled with the partner and may not be available yet. You can disconnect any service at any time from the app settings; from then on we stop receiving new data from it.

5. What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing profiling.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models, and we require the same of our AI provider.
  • We do not share your data with other users, except a coach you explicitly connect with.

Coaches. You connect a coach with an invite code and confirm what they will see: your profile, activities (including those from connected devices), notes, perceived effort, thresholds and zones; they can also create and edit your planned workouts. A coach never sees your connections to partner services or your original files. You can disconnect a coach at any time in Profile → Your coaches: access ends immediately, and workouts the coach already created stay in your calendar.

Invitations by email. If you enter someone's email address to invite them as your coach or athlete, we send them a single email from no-reply@timetrial.io with your name and the invitation code. We do not store their address: we keep only a one-way fingerprint (hash) for up to 30 days, to prevent too many invitations to the same person. Please invite only people who expect to hear from you.

6. AI features

The AI coach is optional and is switched on only after you agree in the app; you can withdraw this consent at any time in the app settings. When you use it, we send only the data needed for that request. We never send to the AI provider:

  • data received from Oura;
  • the name of the professional in your recovery sessions;
  • your email address or other contact details.

The AI provider is Anthropic (Claude API). AI requests may be processed in the United States; see section 7 for how transfers are protected. Data is not used to train models.

7. Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage. Your data is stored in the European Union.
  • Brevo (France) — sending the app's automatic emails (account confirmation, password reset, email change) and of the invitations you choose to send. It receives only the recipient's email address, the language of the message and the content of that email; never your training or health data. Data stored in the European Union.
  • Infomaniak (Infomaniak Network SA, Switzerland) — our mailboxes (support@, privacy@ and info@timetrial.io). If you write to us, your message, your email address and our reply are stored there. Switzerland is recognised by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of data protection.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) — domain name, and hosting of the web version of the app (the app's files only: your data is not stored at Cloudflare and travels directly between your browser and Supabase). Cloudflare processes technical connection data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the pages; during testing, access to the web app is restricted with Cloudflare Access, which also receives the email address used to sign in.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — error reports, so we can fix crashes and bugs. A report contains only the type of error, the point in the app’s code where it happened and technical details such as phone model, operating system and app version; never your name, email, IP address, training or health data, or data from connected services. When a report arrives, Sentry derives from the connection only an approximate location (city and country) and then discards the IP address without storing it. Data stored in the European Union (Germany).
  • Apple (Apple Push Notification service) — only if you turn on notifications in the app: delivers notifications to your iPhone. Apple receives a code that identifies the app on your phone and the text of the notification, which only says who acted and what kind of alert it is (for example “Marco commented on your activity”); never workout names, sport, training or health data, or the text of comments. You can turn notifications off at any time in Profile → Notifications or in your phone’s Settings. On Android, notifications will be delivered by Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) with the same limits.
  • Anthropic — AI features only, as described in section 6.
  • Apple App Store, Google Play and a subscription management service — payments and subscriptions. They do not receive your training or health data.

All processors are bound by data processing agreements. When a provider processes data outside the EU (for example in the United States), the transfer is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

8. Rules for data from connected services

For every connected service we receive only the data types you authorise, use them only to provide the features described here, and do not pass them to third parties other than the processors in section 7 and a coach you connect with. When you disconnect a service or delete your account, we delete the data received from it. In addition:

  • Oura: data is shown and analysed in the app but never sent to AI features.
  • WHOOP: data is never used to train or improve any AI or machine-learning model.
  • Garmin: data is used only to show, analyse and plan your training and for the coach you connect with. It is processed only by our hosting provider (Supabase, EU) and is not sent to AI features; if this changes, it will happen only with Garmin's written approval, after we update this policy and only with your explicit consent. Garmin data and anything derived from it are never sold and never used to train, evaluate or improve AI models. You can disconnect Garmin at any time from the app or from Garmin Connect.
  • Polar, Suunto, COROS, Wahoo, Hammerhead, Withings, Concept2, Zwift, Rouvy, MyWhoosh, FORM: used under each partner's API terms, only for your own account and your coach.
  • Strava: only files from an archive you export and upload yourself.

9. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except data we must keep by law (for example invoices, for the period required by Italian tax law). Backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can at any time:

  • access your data and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data and your account;
  • export your data in a reusable format (portability);
  • withdraw your consent, without affecting processing done before;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

You can exercise most rights directly in the app (Profile → Account and data: export, delete account; disconnect services) — see also how to delete your account — or by writing to privacy@timetrial.io. We reply within 30 days.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, each user can access only their own records, access by our staff is limited to what is strictly necessary, and connections to partner platforms use their official, authorised interfaces.

12. Age

Time Trial is intended for people aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger users.

13. Not medical advice

Time Trial provides training information, not medical advice. Consult a doctor before starting a training programme or if you have symptoms.

14. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will announce significant changes in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version. See also our Terms of Use.

7. Chi tratta i dati per nostro conto

  • Supabase — database, autenticazione e archiviazione file. I tuoi dati sono conservati nell'Unione Europea.
  • Brevo (Francia) — invio delle email automatiche dell'app (conferma dell'account, reimpostazione della password, cambio email) e degli inviti che scegli di mandare. Riceve solo l'indirizzo email del destinatario, la lingua del messaggio e il contenuto di quell'email; mai i tuoi dati di allenamento o di salute. Dati conservati nell'Unione Europea.
  • Infomaniak (Infomaniak Network SA, Svizzera) — le nostre caselle email (support@, privacy@ e info@timetrial.io). Se ci scrivi, il tuo messaggio, il tuo indirizzo email e la nostra risposta sono conservati lì. La Svizzera è riconosciuta dalla Commissione Europea come paese con un livello adeguato di protezione dei dati.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) — nome a dominio e pubblicazione della versione web dell'app (solo i file dell'app: i tuoi dati non sono conservati presso Cloudflare e viaggiano direttamente tra il tuo browser e Supabase). Cloudflare tratta dati tecnici di connessione come indirizzo IP e tipo di browser per consegnare e proteggere le pagine; durante la fase di prova l'accesso alla versione web è limitato con Cloudflare Access, che riceve anche l'email usata per entrare.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — segnalazioni degli errori, per correggere blocchi e malfunzionamenti. Una segnalazione contiene solo il tipo di errore, il punto del codice dell’app in cui è avvenuto e dati tecnici come modello del telefono, sistema operativo e versione dell’app; mai il tuo nome, la tua email, l’indirizzo IP, dati di allenamento o di salute, o dati dei servizi collegati. Quando una segnalazione arriva, Sentry ricava dal collegamento solo una posizione approssimativa (città e paese) e poi scarta l’indirizzo IP senza conservarlo. Dati conservati nell’Unione Europea (Germania).
  • Apple (servizio di notifiche push di Apple) — solo se accendi le notifiche nell'app: consegna le notifiche al tuo iPhone. Apple riceve un codice che identifica l'app sul tuo telefono e il testo della notifica, che dice solo chi ha agito e che tipo di avviso è (per esempio “Marco ha commentato la tua attività”); mai nomi degli allenamenti, sport, dati di allenamento o di salute, o il testo dei commenti. Puoi spegnere le notifiche in qualsiasi momento da Profilo → Notifiche o dalle Impostazioni del telefono. Su Android le notifiche saranno consegnate da Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) con gli stessi limiti.
  • Anthropic — solo per le funzioni di AI, come descritto nella sezione 6.
  • Apple App Store, Google Play e un servizio di gestione abbonamenti — per pagamenti e abbonamenti. Non ricevono i tuoi dati di allenamento o di salute.

Tutti i fornitori sono vincolati da accordi sul trattamento dei dati. Quando un fornitore tratta dati fuori dall'UE (ad esempio negli Stati Uniti), il trasferimento è tutelato dalle Clausole Contrattuali Standard UE o dal Data Privacy Framework UE–USA.
